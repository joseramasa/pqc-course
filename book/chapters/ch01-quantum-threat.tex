\chapter{The Quantum Threat to Modern Cryptography}
\label{ch:quantum-threat}

\abstract*{The advent of large-scale quantum computers poses an existential threat to the cryptographic infrastructure underpinning modern digital communications. This chapter introduces the scope of the problem, from the theoretical certainty of Shor's algorithm to the practical urgency of the ``harvest now, decrypt later'' attack model. We establish the timeline for the post-quantum transition and motivate the mathematical journey ahead.}
