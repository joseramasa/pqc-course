\chapter{The Post-Quantum Landscape}
\label{ch:pqc-landscape}

\abstract*{With the quantum threat established, this chapter surveys the families of mathematical problems proposed as quantum-resistant foundations for cryptography. We trace the NIST standardization process from its 2016 call for proposals through the selection of ML-KEM, ML-DSA, and SLH-DSA as federal standards, and examine the complementary roles of post-quantum cryptography and quantum key distribution.}
